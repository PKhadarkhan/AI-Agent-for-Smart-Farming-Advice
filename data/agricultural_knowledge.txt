==============================
CROP INFORMATION — CEREALS
==============================

RICE (Paddy / Dhaan / Vari):
- Season: Kharif (June-November); Rabi (November-April) in southern deltas
- Soil: Clay loam, silty clay; pH 5.5-7.0; tolerates waterlogged conditions
- Water: High requirement 1200-2000 mm; transplanted paddy needs flooded fields
- Temperature: 20-35°C optimal; germination best at 20-25°C
- Fertilizer: NPK 120:60:60 kg/ha; apply urea in 3 splits (basal, tillering, panicle initiation)
- Varieties: IR-64, Swarna Sub-1, MTU-7029, BPT-5204 (Samba Masuri), Telangana Sona, ADT-43
- Yield: 4-6 tonnes/ha under good management
- Diseases: Blast (Pyricularia), Brown Leaf Spot, Sheath Blight, Bacterial Leaf Blight
- Pests: Yellow Stem Borer, Brown Plant Hopper (BPH), Gall Midge, Leaf Folder
- Chemical control: Tricyclazole for blast; Cartap Hydrochloride for stem borer; Buprofezin for BPH
- Biological control: Trichogramma release for stem borer; light traps for moths
- Harvesting: When 80% of grains turn golden; threshing within 48 hours to avoid quality loss
- Post-harvest: Dry to <14% moisture for safe storage; clean storage prevents weevil infestation
- Water-saving: SRI (System of Rice Intensification) can reduce water by 30% while increasing yield

WHEAT (Gehun / Godhuma):
- Season: Rabi (October-March); sow by mid-November for best yield
- Soil: Well-drained loam or clay loam; pH 6.0-7.5; avoid waterlogging
- Temperature: 10-25°C; frost-sensitive at heading stage
- Water: 450-650 mm; 4-6 irrigations at critical stages
- Critical irrigation stages: Crown root initiation (21 DAS), Jointing (45 DAS), Heading, Milky grain
- Fertilizer: NPK 120:60:40 kg/ha; apply full P+K basal; split N at sowing, tillering, jointing
- Varieties: HD-2967, GW-322, Raj-4120, PBW-343, HD-3086 (heat-tolerant)
- Yield: 3-5 tonnes/ha
- Diseases: Rust (Yellow/Brown/Black) — apply Propiconazole; Loose Smut; Powdery Mildew
- Pests: Aphids — apply Dimethoate; Armyworm
- Harvesting: When grain moisture is 14-16%; combine harvesting preferred
- Less water needed: Wheat needs much less water than rice; suitable for water-scarce regions

MAIZE (Corn / Makka / Makai):
- Season: Kharif (June-September); Rabi (October-February) in South India
- Soil: Well-drained sandy loam to clay loam; pH 6.0-7.5; waterlogging kills quickly
- Temperature: 21-30°C; sensitive to frost
- Water: 500-750 mm; most critical at tasseling and silking stages — never stress then
- Fertilizer: NPK 150:75:60 kg/ha; topdress with urea at knee height (30 DAS)
- Varieties: DHM-117, NK-6240, Pioneer-3396, Bioseed-9681, SEEDTECH-2324
- Yield: 5-8 tonnes/ha for hybrid varieties
- Diseases: Downy Mildew (use Metalaxyl), Maydis Leaf Blight, Turcicum Blight
- Pests: Fall Armyworm (FAW) — most serious pest; Stem Borer; Aphids
- FAW identification: Inverted Y-mark on head capsule; frass inside whorl; ragged leaves
- FAW management: Emamectin Benzoate 0.4 g/L or Chlorantraniliprole 0.3 mL/L; Trichogramma release
- Harvesting: Silage at milk/dough stage; grain harvest at full maturity (30-35% moisture)

SORGHUM / JOWAR (Jwari / Jonna):
- Season: Kharif (June-September); Rabi in some areas
- Soil: Black cotton soil, red soil; pH 6.0-8.0; drought-tolerant
- Water: 400-600 mm; one of the most drought-tolerant cereals
- Temperature: 26-34°C; withstands heat better than maize
- Fertilizer: NPK 80:40:40 kg/ha
- Varieties: CSH-16, CSH-17, M-35-1, ICSV-745
- Yield: 2-4 tonnes/ha grain; 20-30 tonnes/ha fodder
- Suitable for: Water-scarce regions, black soil, Kharif season
- Pests: Stem Borer, Aphids, Shoot Fly; Midge at flowering
- Control: Cartap for stem borer; Chlorpyrifos for shoot fly

BAJRA / PEARL MILLET (Bajra / Sajja):
- Season: Kharif (June-September)
- Soil: Sandy loam, light soils; pH 6.5-8.0; very drought-tolerant
- Water: 300-500 mm; suitable for arid and semi-arid regions (Rajasthan, Gujarat)
- Temperature: 25-35°C; most heat-tolerant cereal
- Fertilizer: NPK 80:40:40 kg/ha
- Varieties: HHB-67 Improved, Pusa 322, GHB-538, RHB-173
- Yield: 1.5-2.5 tonnes/ha
- Suitable for: Dry, sandy, hot regions; least water requirement among cereals
- Diseases: Downy Mildew (use Metalaxyl seed treatment)

RAGI / FINGER MILLET (Ragi / Nachni):
- Season: Kharif (June-September)
- Soil: Sandy loam to clay loam; pH 5.5-7.5; acidic soils tolerated
- Water: 500-800 mm; moderate drought tolerance
- Fertilizer: NPK 60:30:30 kg/ha
- Varieties: GPU-28, MR-6, HR-911, VR-708
- Yield: 2-3 tonnes/ha
- Nutritional value: Rich in calcium, iron, and amino acids — excellent for food security
- Suitable for: Hilly areas, tribal regions, red/laterite soils

BARLEY (Jau):
- Season: Rabi (October-February)
- Soil: Sandy loam to loam; pH 6.5-8.0; tolerates saline soils better than wheat
- Water: 300-450 mm; one of the least water-intensive Rabi crops
- Temperature: 10-20°C
- Fertilizer: NPK 60:40:20 kg/ha
- Suitable for: Saline-sodic lands, low-rainfall Rabi areas

==============================
CROP INFORMATION — PULSES
==============================

ARHAR / TUR / PIGEON PEA (Kandi Pappu / Toor Dal):
- Season: Kharif (June-October); long-duration varieties till December
- Soil: Well-drained loam to sandy loam; pH 6.5-7.5; red and black soils
- Water: 600-1000 mm; moderate drought tolerance once established
- Temperature: 26-30°C
- Fertilizer: NPK 25:50:0 kg/ha; Rhizobium seed treatment (fixes atmospheric N)
- Varieties: ICPL-87119 (Asha), BDN-711, BSMR-736, Pusa Ageti
- Yield: 1.5-2 tonnes/ha
- Diseases: Fusarium Wilt (use wilt-resistant varieties); Sterility Mosaic Virus
- Pests: Helicoverpa pod borer — most serious; Leaf Webber; Pod-sucking bugs
- Control: HaNPV for Helicoverpa; Indoxacarb; Quinalphos for pod sucking bugs
- Intercropping: Pigeon pea + Sorghum (4:2) for drought-prone areas
- Benefits: Fixes nitrogen, improves soil fertility; leaves are good fodder

GRAM / CHICKPEA (Chana / Senaga Pappu):
- Season: Rabi (October-November); harvested March-April
- Soil: Medium-deep well-drained loam; pH 6.0-8.0; black and alluvial soils
- Water: 350-500 mm; very drought-tolerant; grown on residual soil moisture
- Temperature: 15-25°C; frost-tolerant in vegetative stage
- Fertilizer: NPK 20:40:20 kg/ha; Rhizobium seed treatment
- Varieties: JG-11, Vijay, Phule G-5, Vihar, H-208
- Yield: 1-1.5 tonnes/ha dryland; 2-2.5 tonnes/ha irrigated
- Diseases: Wilt & Root Rot (Fusarium) — use wilt-tolerant varieties
- Pests: Helicoverpa pod borer — critical; Cutworm; Aphids
- Control: Spray Emamectin Benzoate at pod-fill; use chickpea NPV

MOONG / GREEN GRAM (Pesara Pappu / Moong Dal):
- Season: Kharif and Zaid (short duration: 60-70 days)
- Soil: Sandy loam; well-drained; pH 6.0-7.5
- Water: 350-500 mm; suited for dry conditions
- Temperature: 25-35°C
- Yield: 0.8-1.2 tonnes/ha
- Advantages: Short duration, fits between kharif and rabi; adds nitrogen to soil
- Suitable for: Post-kharif or post-rabi slot; summer crop

URAD / BLACK GRAM (Minumulu / Urad Dal):
- Season: Kharif, Zaid, Rabi in some states
- Soil: Loam to clay loam; pH 6.0-7.5
- Water: 500-700 mm; moderate requirement
- Fertilizer: NPK 20:40:20 kg/ha; Rhizobium seed treatment
- Yield: 0.6-1.0 tonnes/ha

MASOOR / LENTIL:
- Season: Rabi (October-November to March)
- Soil: Loam to clay loam; pH 6.0-8.0
- Water: 300-450 mm; very drought-tolerant Rabi pulse
- Temperature: 15-25°C
- Yield: 0.8-1.2 tonnes/ha
- Suitable for: Low-water Rabi season; can grow on residual moisture

CROP INFORMATION — OILSEEDS
==============================

GROUNDNUT / PEANUT (Verusenaga / Moongphali):
- Season: Kharif (June-October); Rabi (November-March) in Andhra, Karnataka
- Soil: Sandy loam, red loam; pH 6.0-7.0; well-drained essential (pods rot in waterlogged)
- Water: 500-700 mm; moderate drought-tolerance once established
- Temperature: 25-30°C; peg/pod development needs warm soil (>20°C)
- Fertilizer: NPK 25:50:40 kg/ha; Gypsum 400 kg/ha applied at peg initiation
- Varieties: TAG-24, K-6, ICGS-44, GPBD-4, Kadiri-6, JL-24
- Yield: 2-3 tonnes/ha (pods)
- Diseases: Early Leaf Spot (Cercospora), Late Leaf Spot, Rust, Stem Rot (Collar Rot/Sclerotinia)
- Pests: Leaf Miner, Red Hairy Caterpillar, White Grub (soil pest), Aphids, Thrips
- Control: Carbendazim for leaf spot; Chlorpyrifos soil drench for white grub; Trichoderma viride for collar rot
- Suitability: Excellent for red soil areas; drought-tolerant; good cash crop

SOYBEAN (Soya / Soya Chikkudu):
- Season: Kharif only (June-September); 95-100 day duration
- Soil: Well-drained medium black soil; pH 6.0-7.5; avoid saline/waterlogged
- Water: 450-700 mm; sensitive to waterlogging at any stage
- Temperature: 25-30°C; suitable for Vidarbha, Malwa, Marathwada
- Fertilizer: NPK 30:60:40 kg/ha; Rhizobium + PSB seed treatment
- Varieties: JS-335, NRC-7, MACS-450, Phule Kalyani, RVS-18
- Yield: 2-2.5 tonnes/ha
- Diseases: Bacterial Pustule, Pod & Stem Blight, Yellow Mosaic Virus (YMV)
- Pests: Girdle Beetle, Stem Fly, Semilooper, Whitefly (YMV vector)
- Control: Triazophos for girdle beetle; Imidacloprid seed treatment for whitefly/YMV

MUSTARD (Sarson / Avalu):
- Season: Rabi (October-November); short duration 100-110 days
- Soil: Loam to sandy loam; pH 6.0-7.5; well-drained
- Water: 250-400 mm; drought-tolerant; suits rainfed Rabi areas
- Temperature: 10-25°C
- Fertilizer: NPK 80:40:40 kg/ha
- Varieties: Pusa Bold, Pusa Jagannath, RH-749, Kranti
- Yield: 1.5-2 tonnes/ha
- Pests: Aphids — most serious; Painted Bug; Sawfly
- Control: Dimethoate or Imidacloprid for aphids
- Suitable for: North Indian Rabi; Rajasthan, UP, Haryana; less water than wheat

SUNFLOWER (Surajmukhi):
- Season: Kharif or Rabi; day-neutral crop; can be grown year-round
- Soil: Well-drained loam to sandy loam; pH 6.5-8.0; tolerates mild alkalinity
- Water: 600-800 mm; drought-tolerant but critical at flowering
- Temperature: 20-30°C
- Fertilizer: NPK 90:60:30 kg/ha; Boron 1 kg/ha at pre-flowering
- Yield: 1.5-2 tonnes/ha
- Suitable for: Diverse soils and seasons; less insect pressure than soybean

CASTOR (Arandi / Arandee):
- Season: Kharif (June-August)
- Soil: Sandy loam, deep red soil; pH 6.0-8.0; drought-tolerant
- Water: 400-600 mm; one of the most drought-tolerant oilseeds
- Suitable for: Rainfed dryland areas; Telangana, Gujarat, Rajasthan

SESAME / TIL (Nuvvulu / Til):
- Season: Kharif; short duration 75-90 days
- Soil: Sandy loam, well-drained; pH 6.0-7.5
- Water: 300-500 mm; drought-tolerant
- Suitable for: Light soils; short season; low water requirement

==============================
CROP INFORMATION — VEGETABLES
==============================

TOMATO (Tamata / Tamatar):
- Season: Kharif, Rabi, Zaid; year-round in protected cultivation
- Soil: Well-drained sandy loam to clay loam; pH 6.0-7.0
- Temperature: 18-27°C day; night temperature below 13°C causes poor fruit set
- Water: 400-600 mm; drip irrigation preferred; avoid overhead irrigation
- Fertilizer: NPK 150:75:75 kg/ha; Calcium Nitrate foliar spray at fruiting to prevent BER
- Varieties: Arka Vikas, Pusa Ruby, Arka Ahuti, Swaroopa, Hybrid varieties
- Yield: 20-30 tonnes/ha open-field; 60-80 tonnes/ha protected cultivation
- Diseases: Early Blight (Alternaria) — apply Mancozeb; Late Blight (Phytophthora) — Metalaxyl-M; Tomato Leaf Curl Virus (TLCV); Fusarium Wilt
- Pests: Fruit Borer (Helicoverpa armigera) — most damaging; Whitefly (TLCV vector); Leaf Miner; Spider Mites
- Control for fruit borer: Spinosad 45 SC @ 0.3 mL/L; HaNPV; pheromone traps; hand-pick young larvae
- Control for whitefly: Yellow sticky traps; Buprofezin; Imidacloprid; reflective silver mulch
- Control for mites: Abamectin 1.8 EC @ 0.5 mL/L; Propargite; neem oil
- Biological control: Trichogramma for fruit borer; Chrysoperla against whitefly/mites
- Harvesting: At breaker to pink stage for distant markets; red-ripe for local sale
- Storage: 10-15°C for 2-3 weeks; avoid below 10°C (chilling injury)

CHILLI (Mirchi / Mirch / Karam):
- Season: Kharif (transplant July-August); Rabi (transplant October-November)
- Soil: Sandy loam to clay loam; pH 6.0-7.5; good drainage essential; avoid alkaline soils
- Temperature: 20-30°C; cold stunts growth below 15°C; heat above 35°C causes flower drop
- Fertilizer: NPK 120:60:60 kg/ha; split N in 3 doses; foliar spray of 0.5% ZnSO4
- Varieties: LCA-305, Pusa Jwala, Guntur Sannam (G4/G5), Byadgi Kaddi, Khammam Local
- Yield: Dry chilli 2-3 tonnes/ha; Green chilli 15-25 tonnes/ha
- Diseases: Anthracnose/Die-back (Colletotrichum) — Hexaconazole; Phytophthora Blight — Metalaxyl; Mosaic Virus; Bacterial Leaf Spot
- Pests: Thrips (major sucking pest); Mites; Fruit Borer; Aphids
- Thrips management: Acephate 75 SP; Spinosad; Fipronil; avoid broad-spectrum insecticides
- Mites management: Abamectin 1.8 EC; Propargite; 2-3 sprays at 7-day intervals
- Harvesting: Green stage at 70-90 DAS; dry chilli 120-150 DAS
- Storage: Dry to <8% moisture before storage; fumigation with Aluminium Phosphide in bulk storage

ONION (Pyaaz / Ullipayalu):
- Season: Kharif (June-July transplant); Rabi (Oct-Nov transplant, major crop)
- Soil: Well-drained loam; pH 6.0-7.5; friable; avoid heavy clay
- Temperature: 13-24°C for bulb development; 25-35°C for germination
- Water: 350-550 mm; drip preferred; critical at bulbing stage; reduce water before harvest
- Fertilizer: NPK 100:50:50 kg/ha; split N over 3 doses
- Varieties: Nasik Red, N-2-4-1, Bhima Raj, Bhima Shakti, Agrifound Dark Red
- Yield: 20-25 tonnes/ha Rabi; 15-20 tonnes/ha Kharif
- Diseases: Purple Blotch (Alternaria porri) — Iprodione; Stemphylium Blight; Downy Mildew
- Pests: Thrips — main pest; causes silvery streaks; Onion Fly; Leaf Miner
- Thrips management: Spinosad; Emamectin Benzoate; reflective mulch; intercrop with carrot
- Harvesting: When 50-75% of tops fall over; lift and cure 10-15 days in shade
- Storage: Cool dry well-ventilated shed; temperature 25-35°C; reduce humidity

POTATO (Aloo / Alugadda):
- Season: Rabi (October-December transplant) in plains; April-June hills
- Soil: Sandy loam, well-drained loam; pH 5.5-7.0; friable for tuber development
- Temperature: 15-20°C optimal for tuberisation; stops if >30°C
- Water: 400-600 mm; critical at stolon initiation and tuber enlargement
- Fertilizer: NPK 200:100:200 kg/ha; high potassium essential
- Varieties: Kufri Jyoti, Kufri Pukhraj, Kufri Sindhuri, Kufri Bahar
- Yield: 20-25 tonnes/ha
- Diseases: Late Blight (Phytophthora) — most serious; apply Mancozeb preventively; Early Blight; Common Scab
- Pests: Aphids (virus vectors); Potato Tuber Moth in storage
- Storage: Cool dark conditions 4-10°C; sprout inhibition with CIPC
- Suitable for: Punjab, UP, Bihar, MP hills; cool season

BRINJAL / EGGPLANT (Vankai / Baingan):
- Season: Kharif, Rabi; year-round planting possible
- Soil: Sandy loam to clay loam; pH 5.5-7.0; wide adaptability
- Temperature: 21-30°C
- Water: 500-700 mm; moderate requirement
- Fertilizer: NPK 100:50:50 kg/ha
- Diseases: Phomopsis Blight; Little Leaf (mycoplasma) — no cure, rogue out
- Pests: Brinjal Shoot & Fruit Borer (BSFB) — most serious; Leaf-Roller; Flea Beetle
- BSFB management: Spinosad; Emamectin Benzoate; Bt spray; destroy infested shoots

CUCUMBER (Kheera / Dosakaya):
- Season: Zaid (Feb-April); Kharif
- Soil: Sandy loam; pH 6.0-7.5
- Water: 400-600 mm; drip preferred; needs regular moisture
- Temperature: 25-35°C; frost-sensitive
- Fertilizer: NPK 100:60:80 kg/ha
- Suitable for: Summer season; low-water requirement relative to other cucurbits; fits Zaid slot

WATERMELON (Tarbuz):
- Season: Zaid (February-May)
- Soil: Sandy loam, well-drained; pH 6.0-7.0
- Water: 400-600 mm; drip or furrow irrigation; less water than other vegetables
- Temperature: 25-35°C; needs heat for sugar development
- Suitable for: Sandy soil, summer season, less water regions
- Yield: 25-40 tonnes/ha

CROP INFORMATION — CASH CROPS
==============================

COTTON (Kapas / Patti):
- Season: Kharif (May planting to December harvest); 180-200 day crop
- Soil: Deep black cotton soil (Vertisol) ideal; red loam also suitable; pH 7.0-8.5
- Temperature: 25-35°C; sensitive to frost and waterlogging
- Water: 700-1200 mm; critical at squaring, flowering, boll formation
- Fertilizer: NPK 120:60:60 kg/ha; foliar Boron 0.1% at flowering; avoid excess N (vegetative growth)
- Varieties: Bt Cotton hybrid (most popular); DCH-32; NHH-44
- Yield: Seed cotton 2-3 tonnes/ha; lint ~35% of seed cotton weight
- Diseases: Fusarium Wilt (soil-borne; use resistant Bt hybrids); Bacterial Blight; Alternaria Leaf Spot
- Pests: Bollworm complex (Pink, American, Spotted) — Bt cotton controls; Whitefly; Thrips; Jassids; Aphids; Pink Bollworm (resurgence)
- Whitefly management: Yellow sticky traps; Buprofezin 25 WP; Diafenthiuron; Spiromesifen
- Thrips management: Spinosad; Fipronil 5 SC; neem-based sprays
- Pink bollworm management: Pheromone traps for monitoring; Chlorantraniliprole; destroy crop residue
- Harvesting: 3-4 pickings; first at 170-180 DAS; avoid picking in high humidity (staining)
- Suitable for: Black soil regions — Maharashtra, Telangana, AP, Gujarat, MP

SUGARCANE (Cheruku / Ganna):
- Season: Plant cane February-March; ratoon annually; 12-18 month duration
- Soil: Deep loam, clay loam; pH 6.5-7.5; good drainage essential
- Temperature: 20-35°C; frost kills cane
- Water: High 1500-2500 mm; drip irrigation improves WUE by 30%
- Fertilizer: NPK 250:100:120 kg/ha split over the season; apply FYM 20 t/ha
- Varieties: Co-86032, Co-0238, CoJ-64, CoA-7602, Co-94012
- Yield: 70-100 tonnes/ha; sugar recovery 10-12%
- Diseases: Red Rot (Colletotrichum) — most serious; Smut; Grassy Shoot (phytoplasma); Wilt
- Pests: Stem Borer; Pyrilla (Sugarcane leafhopper); Whitefly; Scale Insect; Root Borer
- Control: Release Trichogramma for stem borer; Dimethoate for Pyrilla; soil drench for root borer
- Suitable for: Fertile deep soils; UP, Maharashtra, AP, Karnataka, TN; needs abundant water or irrigation

TURMERIC (Pasupu / Haldi):
- Season: May-June planting; harvested after 8-9 months (January-March)
- Soil: Loamy, well-drained; pH 6.0-7.5; cannot tolerate waterlogging
- Temperature: 20-30°C; high humidity favours rhizome growth
- Water: 1500 mm well-distributed; mulching (paddy straw) essential
- Fertilizer: NPK 60:50:120 kg/ha; high potassium for rhizome development; FYM 40 t/ha
- Varieties: Duggirala, Tekurpet, Erode Local, Alleppey Finger, Rajapuri, Sughandham
- Yield: Fresh rhizome 25-30 tonnes/ha; dry 5-6 tonnes/ha
- Diseases: Leaf Blotch (Colletotrichum) — Mancozeb; Rhizome Rot (Pythium) — Metalaxyl drench
- Pests: Rhizome Scale (Aspidiella hartii); Shoot Borer (Conogethes punctiferalis); Leaf Roller
- Shoot borer management: Chlorpyrifos 2.5 mL/L soil drench; remove and destroy infested shoots
- Rhizome scale: Dip rhizomes in Chlorpyrifos solution before planting
- Important markets: Nizamabad (AP/Telangana); Erode (TN); Sangli (Maharashtra)

JUTE:
- Season: Kharif (March-June); harvested August-September
- Soil: Alluvial; pH 6.0-7.5; needs good moisture
- Water: 1000-1500 mm; suited to Bengal, Bihar, Assam flood plains

==============================
SOIL TYPES AND CROP SUITABILITY
==============================

BLACK COTTON SOIL (Vertisol / Regur Soil):
- Regions: Maharashtra (Vidarbha, Marathwada), Telangana, AP, MP, Gujarat
- pH: 7.0-8.5; self-mulching (swells when wet, cracks when dry)
- Properties: High clay content (montmorillonite); rich in Ca, Mg; low in N, P, organic matter
- Best crops: Cotton (most suitable), Wheat, Jowar/Sorghum, Sunflower, Soybean, Chickpea
- Good crops: Maize, Bajra, Linseed, Safflower
- Poor crops: Rice (waterlogging risk from swelling), Root vegetables
- Management:
  * Deep ploughing once every 3 years to break hard pan
  * Raised beds (BBF — Broad Bed Furrow) for drainage during Kharif
  * Apply Gypsum 2.5 t/ha to improve structure of alkaline vertisols
  * Add organic matter (FYM) to improve aeration and nutrient availability

RED SOIL (Laterite / Alfisol):
- Regions: AP, Telangana, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Odisha
- pH: 5.5-7.0; reddish due to iron oxide; low in N, P, organic matter
- Properties: Light, porous, well-drained; low water retention; leaches nutrients quickly
- Best crops: Groundnut (most suitable), Sorghum, Millets, Pulses (arhar, moong, gram)
- Good crops: Cotton, Maize, Castor, Sesame, Cowpea
- Poor crops: Rice (needs more water retention), Wheat (needs heavier soil)
- Management:
  * Add lime if pH < 5.5 (neutralise acidity)
  * Heavy FYM application (15-20 t/ha) to improve water and nutrient retention
  * Mulching to reduce evaporation
  * Frequent light irrigation due to low water retention

ALLUVIAL SOIL (Inceptisol / Entisol):
- Regions: Indo-Gangetic plains (UP, Punjab, Haryana, Bihar), river deltas (Bengal, Andhra)
- pH: 6.5-8.0; very fertile; good water retention; periodic replenishment by floods
- Properties: Loamy to silty loam; contains balanced N, P, K; best agricultural soils
- Best crops: Rice, Wheat, Sugarcane, Vegetables (all types), Banana, Jute, Maize
- Management: Regular NPK to replace nutrients removed by intensive cropping; avoid compaction

SANDY LOAM SOIL:
- pH: 6.0-7.0; good aeration and drainage; low water retention
- Best crops: Groundnut, Ginger, Watermelon, Cucumber, Pulses, Tobacco
- Poor crops: Rice, Sugarcane (need more water retention)
- Management: Organic matter; mulching; drip irrigation; frequent but light irrigation

CLAY SOIL:
- pH: 6.0-7.5; very high water retention; poor drainage; slow to warm
- Best crops: Rice (waterlogging-tolerant), Wheat, Sugarcane
- Poor crops: Groundnut, Root crops (difficult harvesting)
- Management: Raised beds for drainage; add sand/organic matter; avoid machinery when wet

LOAMY SOIL (best general purpose):
- pH: 6.0-7.0; balanced texture; excellent for most crops
- Best crops: Nearly all crops — wheat, maize, cotton, vegetables, pulses, oilseeds
- Management: Maintain organic matter; rotate crops; balanced fertilization

SALINE-SODIC SOILS:
- EC > 4 dS/m (saline); SAR > 13 (sodic/alkali)
- Reclamation of saline soils: Leach with good water; grow salt-tolerant crops
- Reclamation of sodic soils: Apply Gypsum 5-10 t/ha; sub-surface drainage
- Salt-tolerant crops: Barley (best), Rice, Cotton, Sugarbeet, Dhaincha (green manure)
- Avoid: Groundnut, Soybean, Beans in saline soils

==============================
SEASONAL CALENDAR — INDIA
==============================

KHARIF SEASON (Rainy Season):
- Onset: June (with monsoon); sowing June-July
- Harvest: September-November
- Duration: 90-180 days depending on crop
- Key crops: Rice, Maize, Cotton, Soybean, Groundnut, Sorghum (Jowar), Bajra, Arhar/Tur, Urad, Moong, Sesame
- Characteristics: Depends on monsoon rainfall; high pest pressure due to humidity; waterlogging risk
- Pre-season activities: Field preparation by May; bund repair; drainage channels; buy seeds by May
- Suitable soils: Black, red, alluvial — all types depending on crop

RABI SEASON (Winter Season):
- Onset: October-November; sowing October-November
- Harvest: March-April
- Duration: 90-150 days
- Key crops: Wheat (main crop), Barley, Mustard, Gram/Chickpea, Masoor/Lentil, Peas, Sunflower
- Characteristics: Cool temperatures; relies on stored soil moisture + irrigation; lower pest pressure than Kharif
- Suitable regions: North and Central India; irrigated areas
- Activities: Soil preparation September; sowing November; irrigation at critical stages

ZAID SEASON (Summer/Short Season):
- Onset: February-March; sowing March-April
- Harvest: May-June
- Duration: 60-90 days
- Key crops: Watermelon, Muskmelon, Cucumber, Short-duration Moong, Fodder crops, Vegetables
- Characteristics: Hot and dry; requires irrigation; short window
- Suitable for: Farmers wanting an extra crop between Rabi harvest and Kharif sowing

SEASON-BASED RECOMMENDATIONS (by current month):
- January: Rabi crops in vegetative/reproductive stage; wheat irrigation critical; guard against frost
- February: Wheat heading; Rabi pulse harvest begins; start summer crop land preparation
- March: Zaid season starts; vegetables, watermelon, moong; harvest Rabi crops
- April: Zaid crops growing; summer heat stress management; repair bunds before monsoon
- May: Pre-kharif preparation; buy Kharif seeds; field bund repair; drainage channels
- June: Kharif sowing starts with monsoon onset; rice transplanting; sow cotton, maize
- July: Kharif crops growing; watch for pests; ensure drainage; apply fertilizers
- August: Peak Kharif; high pest/disease pressure; spray fungicides if needed
- September: Late Kharif; start Rabi land preparation after rain; harvest early Kharif crops
- October: Kharif harvest; Rabi sowing starts; wheat, chickpea, mustard sowing
- November: Rabi growing; wheat CRI irrigation; apply phosphorus before sowing
- December: Rabi crops growing; cold protection; monitor aphids in mustard/wheat

==============================
INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT (IPM)
==============================

PRINCIPLES OF IPM (in order of preference):
1. PREVENTION: Use certified disease-free seed; crop rotation; resistant varieties; field sanitation
2. CULTURAL CONTROL: Deep summer ploughing; removal of crop debris; trap crops; intercropping
3. BIOLOGICAL CONTROL: Natural enemies, bioagents, biopesticides
4. CHEMICAL CONTROL: Only when pest population crosses Economic Threshold Level (ETL); use selective insecticides

ECONOMIC THRESHOLD LEVELS (ETL):
- Brown Plant Hopper (BPH) in rice: 10-20 hoppers/hill
- Fall Armyworm (FAW) in maize: 5% damage or 1 egg mass per m²
- Helicoverpa in cotton: 1-2 eggs/plant or >5% damaged fruiting bodies
- Aphids in wheat: 8-10 aphids/tiller at heading

COMMON BIOLOGICAL CONTROLS:
- Trichogramma chilonis (1 lakh cards/ha): egg parasitoid for stem borer, fruit borer, FAW
- Chrysoperla carnea (predator): effective against aphids, whitefly, thrips, mites
- Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt): caterpillar pests — HANPVfor Helicoverpa; SpNPV for FAW
- Beauveria bassiana (fungal biopesticide): whitefly, thrips, aphids; spray in cool, humid conditions
- Nuclear Polyhedrosis Virus (NPV): HaNPV for Helicoverpa @ 250 LE/ha; SpNPV for Spodoptera
- Trichoderma viride/harzianum: soil-borne diseases — collar rot, wilt, root rot; mix with FYM

NEEM-BASED PRODUCTS:
- NSKE (Neem Seed Kernel Extract) 5%: broad-spectrum repellent and antifeedant; spray 3-4 times
- Neem oil 3% EC: fungicide + insecticide; effective against sucking pests; weekly spray
- Azadirachtin 0.15% EC: for sucking pests, leaf miners, thrips; do not mix with fungicides

PEST-SPECIFIC IDENTIFICATION AND MANAGEMENT:

FALL ARMYWORM (FAW — Spodoptera frugiperda):
- Crops affected: Maize (primary), sorghum, rice, wheat
- Identification: Inverted Y-mark on head capsule; frass in whorl; window-pane damage; pin-hole leaves
- Chemical: Emamectin Benzoate 5 SG @ 0.4 g/L; Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 0.3 mL/L
- Biological: Release Trichogramma (2x); apply SpNPV; spray Bt at early instar
- Cultural: Deep summer ploughing; early sowing; overhead irrigation to dislodge larvae
- Safety: Spray in early morning or evening; avoid spraying during flowering

BROWN PLANT HOPPER (BPH — Nilaparvata lugens):
- Crops: Rice
- Identification: Hopperburn — circular brown patches in field; honeydew deposits; insects at base of plant
- Chemical: Buprofezin 25 WP @ 1 g/L; Pymetrozine 50 WG @ 0.3 g/L
- AVOID: Monocrotophos, Methyl Parathion — cause BPH resurgence
- Biological: Preserve natural enemies (spiders, mirid bugs); avoid broad-spectrum insecticides

HELICOVERPA ARMIGERA (American Bollworm / Tomato Fruit Borer):
- Crops: Cotton, Tomato, Chickpea, Pigeonpea
- Identification: Circular bore hole in fruit/boll; frass outside; larva inside
- Chemical: Emamectin Benzoate 5 SG; Indoxacarb 15.8 EC; Chlorantraniliprole
- Biological: HaNPV @ 250 LE/ha; Bt (Bacillus thuringiensis var. kurstaki) spray
- Cultural: Pheromone traps (1 trap/acre for monitoring); hand-pick early-stage larvae
- Safety: Use protective gear; spray in morning; avoid drift to flowering crops

WHITEFLY (Bemisia tabaci):
- Crops: Cotton, Tomato, Chilli, Vegetables, Legumes
- Role: Direct feeding (silvery leaves) + virus vector (TLCV in tomato, CLCuV in cotton)
- Identification: Tiny white insects under leaves; yellowing; sooty mould
- Chemical: Buprofezin 25 WP; Diafenthiuron 50 WP; Spiromesifen 240 SC
- Biological: Beauveria bassiana spray; yellow sticky traps (1 per 10 m²); reflective mulch
- Cultural: Remove volunteer plants; weed-free field border; avoid late sowing

APHIDS (Machi / Maahu):
- Crops: Wheat, Mustard, Potato, Chilli, Cotton
- Identification: Clusters of soft-bodied insects; honeydew; ants around colonies
- Chemical: Dimethoate 30 EC @ 1 mL/L; Imidacloprid 17.8 SL @ 0.5 mL/L
- Biological: Chrysoperla carnea; Coccinellid beetles (ladybird — preserve); Banker plants

STEM BORER (Chilo partellus and Sesamia species):
- Crops: Maize, Sorghum, Rice, Sugarcane
- Identification: Dead heart in vegetative stage; white ear in reproductive stage
- Chemical: Chlorpyrifos 20 EC @ 2.5 mL/L in whorl; Cartap Hydrochloride
- Biological: Trichogramma chilonis release @ 1 lakh/ha; Cotesia flavipes release

THRIPS:
- Crops: Chilli, Cotton, Onion, Groundnut
- Identification: Silvery-bronze streaks on leaves; scarring; leaf curl in chilli
- Chemical: Spinosad 45 SC @ 0.3 mL/L; Fipronil 5 SC @ 2 mL/L; Acephate 75 SP
- Biological: Blue sticky traps; Amblyseius cucumeris (predatory mite)

==============================
DISEASE MANAGEMENT
==============================

GENERAL DISEASE PREVENTION:
1. Use certified pathogen-free seed or treated seed
2. Crop rotation — break disease cycle (avoid same crop family consecutively)
3. Soil solarisation — kills soil-borne pathogens (effective in summer)
4. Remove and destroy infected plant parts — do not compost diseased material
5. Manage drainage — most fungal diseases need standing water
6. Resistant varieties — best long-term strategy

FUNGAL DISEASES:
- Blast in rice: Tricyclazole 75 WP @ 0.6 g/L; Isoprothiolane 40 EC @ 1.5 mL/L
- Rust in wheat (yellow/brown/black): Propiconazole 25 EC @ 1 mL/L; Tebuconazole
- Downy mildew (maize, grapevine): Metalaxyl-M + Mancozeb; improve drainage
- Late blight of potato/tomato: Mancozeb 75 WP (preventive); Metalaxyl-M + Mancozeb (curative)
- Early blight: Chlorothalonil; Copper oxychloride
- Anthracnose (chilli, mango, beans): Hexaconazole 5 SC; Carbendazim 50 WP
- Wilt diseases (Fusarium, Pythium): Carbendazim soil drench; Trichoderma viride in soil
- Powdery mildew: Sulphur 80 WP; Hexaconazole; Triadimefon

BACTERIAL DISEASES:
- Bacterial leaf blight of rice: Copper bactericide; resistant varieties (BPT-5204 moderately tolerant)
- Bacterial leaf spot of chilli: Copper-based fungicide; avoid overhead irrigation

VIRAL DISEASES:
- Tomato Leaf Curl Virus (TLCV): No cure; control whitefly vector; rogue infected plants
- Yellow Mosaic Virus in soybean/beans: No cure; control whitefly; use resistant varieties
- Cotton Leaf Curl Virus (CLCuV): Remove infected plants; control whitefly; Bt cotton with resistance
- Prevention tip: All viral diseases are insect-vectored — manage the vector insect

==============================
FERTILIZER AND SOIL NUTRITION
==============================

MACRONUTRIENTS:
- Nitrogen (N): Promotes leaf/stem growth; apply as Urea (46% N) or DAP (18-46%)
  * Split application: Basal (25%) + Tillering/Branching (50%) + Panicle/Flowering (25%)
  * Excess N: lush growth, more pest attacks, lodging
  * Deficiency: yellowing of older leaves; stunted growth
- Phosphorus (P): Root development and flowering; apply as SSP (16% P) or DAP
  * Apply as basal dose before sowing; relatively immobile in soil
  * Deficiency: Purplish discolouration of leaves; poor root development
- Potassium (K): Disease resistance, drought tolerance, quality; apply as MOP (60% K)
  * Critical for tuber/rhizome/fruit crops — potato, sugarcane, turmeric, banana
  * Deficiency: Brown scorching of leaf margins

SECONDARY AND MICRONUTRIENTS:
- Calcium: Cell wall strength; apply Calcium Nitrate or gypsum; prevents BER in tomato
- Sulphur: Protein synthesis; apply Gypsum (18% S) or elemental sulphur; important for pulses
- Zinc (Zn): Most common deficiency in Indian soils; khaira disease in rice; apply ZnSO4 25 kg/ha
- Iron (Fe): Chlorosis in alkaline soils; apply FeSO4 0.5% foliar spray
- Boron (B): Flower/fruit setting; critical for cotton and sunflower; apply Borax 1 kg/ha
- Manganese (Mn): Deficiency in alkaline paddy soils; apply MnSO4 foliar spray

ORGANIC NUTRITION:
- Farmyard Manure (FYM): 10-15 t/ha; improves soil structure and water retention
- Vermicompost: 3-5 t/ha; slow-release nutrients; excellent for vegetables
- Green Manure crops: Dhaincha (Sesbania), Sunhemp; incorporate at flowering before main crop
- Biofertilizers: Rhizobium (legumes N-fixation); PSB (phosphate solubilizing bacteria); Azospirillum (grass crops); Azotobacter (non-legumes)
- Combine organic + inorganic: Integrated Plant Nutrition for best results and soil health

SOIL TESTING AND FERTILIZER RECOMMENDATION:
- Soil test every 2-3 years from KVK or agricultural office
- Soil Health Card provides crop-specific recommendations
- Test for: pH, EC, N, P, K, organic carbon, micronutrients (Zn, B, Fe, Mn)
- Apply lime if pH < 6.0 (1-2 t/ha); Gypsum if pH > 8.0 or sodic

==============================
WATER MANAGEMENT AND IRRIGATION
==============================

DRIP IRRIGATION:
- Crops: Vegetables, Fruit crops, Cotton, Sugarcane, Chilli, Banana
- Water saving: 40-60% compared to flood irrigation
- Benefits: Uniform moisture; fertigation (fertilizer through drip); less weed growth
- Government subsidy: PM Krishi Sinchai Yojana; National Horticulture Mission

SPRINKLER IRRIGATION:
- Crops: Wheat, Groundnut, Pulses, Vegetables on undulating terrain
- Water saving: 25-40%; good for light soils with fast drainage

CRITICAL IRRIGATION STAGES (important to never skip):
- Rice: Transplanting; Tillering; Panicle initiation; Grain filling
- Wheat: Crown root initiation (21 DAS); Jointing (45 DAS); Heading; Grain filling (Milky)
- Maize: Tasseling + Silking — most critical; 5 days of drought here reduces yield by 50%
- Cotton: Squaring; Flowering; Boll development
- Groundnut: Peg initiation; Pod development
- Sugarcane: Formative period (April-July)

LOW-WATER CROPS (suitable for water-scarce areas):
- Bajra (Pearl Millet): 300-500 mm; most drought-tolerant cereal
- Sorghum (Jowar): 400-600 mm; drought-tolerant; good for black soil
- Ragi (Finger Millet): 500-800 mm; grows in acidic soils
- Castor: 400-600 mm; drought-tolerant oilseed
- Moong: 350-500 mm; short duration; low water requirement
- Lentil (Masoor): 300-450 mm; Rabi pulse on residual moisture
- Barley: 300-450 mm; salt-tolerant; less water than wheat
- Chickpea (Gram): 350-500 mm; residual moisture Rabi crop
- Sesame (Til): 300-500 mm; short duration; light soils
- Watermelon: 400-600 mm; hot dry weather; summer season

RAIN WATER HARVESTING:
- Farm ponds (25x25x3 m): collect monsoon runoff for Rabi/Zaid irrigation
- Contour bunds: slow runoff; improve infiltration
- Check dams: small streams, gullies
- Subsurface dykes: trap sub-surface flow

WATER STRESS MANAGEMENT:
- Mulching: Paddy straw mulch 5-8 t/ha; plastic mulch; reduces evaporation by 30-40%
- Antitranspirants: Kaolin 5% foliar spray; wax-based products
- Deep tillage: Sub-soil ploughing improves water storage capacity

==============================
CROP ROTATION AND INTERCROPPING
==============================

BENEFICIAL CROP ROTATIONS:
- Rice → Wheat (Indo-Gangetic plains): classic; wheat benefits from rice residue N
- Cotton → Chickpea or Soybean: breaks wilt disease cycle; adds N from legume
- Maize → Chickpea: excellent — adds nitrogen; different root system
- Sorghum → Safflower (Rabi): deep-rooted rotation; good in black soil
- Groundnut → Sorghum or Bajra: prevents soil-borne disease buildup in peanut
- Sugarcane → Soybean or Onion (in ratoon row): improves income

INTERCROPPING SYSTEMS (for yield stability and income):
- Sorghum + Pigeon Pea (4:2 row ratio): drought-tolerant; stable income; AP/Telangana
- Maize + Black Gram: rain-fed Kharif; legume N benefit
- Groundnut + Sunflower (1:4 rows): mixed income; groundnut benefits from sunflower shade
- Cotton + Greengram between cotton rows: reduces weed; greengram N benefit
- Banana + Elephant Foot Yam: shade; root crop income; South India
- Coconut + Pepper or Cocoa (under canopy): Kerala model

CROP ROTATION PRINCIPLES:
- Alternate cereals with legumes: breaks pest/disease cycle and restores nitrogen
- Avoid same family consecutively: e.g., do not grow tomato-potato-brinjal in same plot every year
- Deep-rooted after shallow-rooted: improves soil structure at different depths

==============================
WEATHER-BASED FARMING GUIDANCE
==============================

MONSOON PREPARATION (May-June):
- Repair field bunds and drainage channels before June
- Pre-sowing irrigation if monsoon delayed beyond June 20
- Store seeds in cool, dry airtight containers; test germination
- Service farm equipment before season

HEAT STRESS MANAGEMENT (> 35°C):
- Irrigate in early morning (6-9 AM) or evening (5-7 PM) — not midday
- Apply mulch (paddy straw 5-8 t/ha) to conserve moisture and reduce soil temperature
- Foliar spray of Kaolin 5% to reduce canopy temperature by 2-3°C
- Avoid transplanting and spraying during peak heat (11 AM-3 PM)
- Heat-tolerant varieties: HD-3086 (wheat); heat-tolerant tomato hybrids

COLD AND FROST PROTECTION (< 10°C):
- Light irrigation before frost event — latent heat released as water freezes protects crop
- Smoke screens in orchards: burning dry biomass on field borders
- Use plastic/polythene mulch or row covers for vegetables and nurseries
- Delay transplanting until daily minimum temperature is above 15°C
- Frost-susceptible stages: wheat heading, tomato flowering, potato tuberisation

DROUGHT AND LOW RAINFALL MANAGEMENT:
- Deep tillage (sub-soil ploughing) for moisture conservation
- Grow drought-tolerant varieties: Ragi, Bajra, Jowar, Castor, Moong, Gram
- Construct farm ponds; use rainwater harvesting
- Intercropping: Sorghum + Pigeon Pea system
- Skip irrigation system: alternate furrow irrigation to save 30% water

HIGH RAINFALL AND WATERLOGGING:
- Clear drainage channels; create field drains at 3-5 m intervals
- Use raised beds (BBF) for sensitive crops — cotton, soybean, chilli
- Drain fields within 24 hours of waterlogging to prevent root suffocation
- Spray Mancozeb to prevent fungal diseases after heavy rain

==============================
HARVESTING AND POST-HARVEST
==============================

OPTIMAL HARVEST INDICATORS:
- Rice: 80% of grains golden-yellow; do not delay beyond 7-10 days of maturity
- Wheat: Grain moisture 14-16% (straw turns golden); use combine if available
- Cotton: Bolls fully open; first picking at 170-180 DAS; 3-4 pickings total
- Tomato: Green to breaker stage for distant markets; red for local sale
- Groundnut: Pods mature (inner wall blackened); harvest in morning to avoid pod shattering
- Onion: 50-75% tops fall naturally; cure 10-15 days before storage

POST-HARVEST HANDLING:
- Dry grains to safe moisture: Rice <14%; Wheat <12%; Maize <12% before storage
- Clean grain: Remove broken, diseased, insect-damaged grain before storage
- Store in proper bins: Metallic silos; CAP storage; HDPE bags; avoid gunny sacks (moisture)
- Fumigation: Aluminium Phosphide tablets in sealed storage for insect control
- Cold storage: Potato (4-10°C); Onion (0-2°C); Tomato (10-15°C); Garlic (0°C)
- Avoid direct ground contact: Use pallets/wooden boards to prevent moisture uptake

VALUE ADDITION:
- Rice: Milling to remove bran; parboiling improves nutrition
- Turmeric: Boiling + drying + polishing; export quality Erode/Nizamabad grade
- Chilli: Solar drying; grinding; oleoresin extraction
- Groundnut: Oil expelling; peanut butter; boiled groundnut for local sale

==============================
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
==============================

Q: Which crop is best for black soil in Kharif season?
A: Cotton is the most traditional and suitable crop for deep black soil (Vertisol) in Kharif. Soybean, Jowar (Sorghum), and Maize are also well-suited. Avoid rice in poorly drained black soil.

Q: Which crop requires the least water?
A: Bajra (Pearl Millet) and Sorghum (Jowar) require the least water among cereals. For pulses, Gram (Chickpea) and Masoor (Lentil) need very little water. Castor is the most drought-tolerant oilseed.

Q: What crop is good for red soil?
A: Groundnut is best suited for red (laterite) soil. Also suitable: Sorghum, Millets, Pulses (arhar, moong, urad), and Cotton (in deep red soils).

Q: What to grow in Rabi season with irrigation?
A: Wheat, Chickpea, Mustard, Sunflower, Onion, Potato, Green Peas.

Q: What to grow in summer/Zaid season?
A: Watermelon, Muskmelon, Cucumber, Moong (Green Gram), Fodder crops.

Q: How do I control tomato fruit borer?
A: Use pheromone traps; spray Spinosad 45 SC @ 0.3 mL/L or HaNPV; Trichogramma release; Emamectin Benzoate. Always wear protective gear while spraying.

Q: What is MSP and how does it help farmers?
A: MSP (Minimum Support Price) is the government-guaranteed floor price for 23 crops. If mandi prices fall below MSP, government agencies buy at MSP. It protects farmers from distress selling.

Q: How do I improve soil health?
A: Get Soil Health Card from KVK. Apply FYM 10-15 t/ha; use biofertilizers; practice crop rotation; avoid burning crop residue; apply lime if acidic.

Q: Which organic methods control pests?
A: NSKE 5% spray; neem oil 3%; release Trichogramma, Chrysoperla, Beauveria; use pheromone traps; remove infected plants; crop rotation.

Q: What fertilizer for rice in Kharif?
A: NPK 120:60:60 kg/ha. Apply full P+K as basal, Urea in 3 splits: at transplanting, tillering (30 DAS), panicle initiation (55-60 DAS). Use Zinc Sulphate 25 kg/ha if zinc deficiency.
